# Left Section

## Introduction

Iterative synthetic-data training can narrow distributional coverage when later rounds are filtered by incomplete reference data.

## Paradigms

Replace paradigm [R1]: train from the previous generated round.

Accumulate paradigm [R2]: train from pooled earlier rounds.

## Theorem Analysis

Theorem 1: selected distributions move toward the filtering preference.

Theorem 2: trace decay gives a compact diversity certificate.

Theorem 3: target-domain error is controlled by distribution mismatch once fitting error is small.
